# What belongs in #news

Edit this file in plain English to tune the filter. No code changes needed.

## Who is reading

The team at Lotus Labs, an onchain credit protocol (tranched lending). They are founders, BD, risk, and engineering people who already live on crypto Twitter. Post only stories a founder of an onchain credit protocol would want to know about today. When in doubt, skip. A good day is 5 to 15 posts.

## Post

- Onchain lending and credit: lending protocols (Aave, Morpho, Euler, Spark, Compound, Fluid, Kamino, Maple, Ajna, Tenor and similar), new versions and launches, fixed-rate and tranched credit, onchain private credit, credit and risk events.
- Vaults and curators: vault launches at major protocols or institutions, curator and risk manager news (Gauntlet, Steakhouse, Re7, Sentora, Veda, kpk and similar), vault data and yield infrastructure.
- DeFi protocol events: shutdowns and wind-downs, exploits and hacks with material losses (especially oracle, collateral, or lending exploits), depegs, major tokenomics or treasury overhauls, token buybacks at notable protocols.
- Stablecoins and yield-bearing dollars: issuer news (Circle, Tether, Ethena, Sky, Paxos, Agora and similar), launches by banks or fintechs, reserve or peg events, stablecoin legislation.
- RWA and tokenization: tokenized treasuries, credit, funds, and stocks; institutional onchain funds (BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, WisdomTree, Apollo, Ondo, Superstate, Securitize, Centrifuge, Grove and similar).
- Crypto regulation: SEC, CFTC, OCC, Fed, Treasury, and Congress actions, rules, exemptions, guidance, or enforcement that touch DeFi, lending, vaults, stablecoins, tokenization, or market structure. Major non-US regimes (EU, UK, Singapore, Hong Kong) only when they change stablecoin or DeFi rules.
- M&A and funding: acquisitions of crypto companies by anyone (crypto, tradfi, or AI companies), raises of roughly $10M or more, and smaller raises in DeFi credit, risk, insurance, or vault infrastructure. Shutdowns of notable crypto companies or media.
- Institutional adoption onchain: banks, brokers, exchanges, and asset managers (Robinhood, Citadel, Anchorage, Galaxy, Kraken, Coinbase, JPMorgan and similar) launching onchain lending, stablecoin, tokenization, or settlement products, or their own chains.
- Onchain insurance, security, and oracle providers: notable launches, acquisitions, or incidents.

## Skip

- Prices and markets: price moves, market recaps, liquidation tallies, daily ETF flows, "BTC hits X", derivatives positioning.
- Memecoins, NFTs, gaming, celebrity or politician tokens, launchpads.
- Technical analysis, price predictions, "analyst says" pieces.
- General tradfi and macro (Fed decisions, equities, earnings) unless the story is directly about crypto.
- Sponsored posts, small-project press releases, airdrops, points campaigns, exchange listings.
- Opinion columns, explainers, podcasts, newsletters, weekly roundups.
- Bitcoin mining, corporate BTC treasury purchases, Strategy buying more bitcoin.
- Crime stories about individuals (scams, arrests, ransoms) unless they involve a DeFi protocol exploit.

## SEC press releases

The SEC feed carries everything the agency publishes. Post only items about crypto, digital assets, tokenization, or onchain markets. Skip everything else, including proxy and shareholder rules, accounting and auditing, non-crypto enforcement and fraud cases, staffing announcements, and administrative notices.
