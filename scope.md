# What belongs in #news

Plain English, edited by hand. The filter reads this file every run, so changing a line here changes what posts. No code changes needed.

## Who is reading

The Lotus Labs team: an institutional onchain credit market, pre-launch. BTC/ETH balance-sheet borrowing first, RWA credit next with Cicada Partners as first vault manager. Thesis: the "missing middle" of DeFi credit, between isolated pools and pooled risk, priced probabilistically. Readers are founders, BD, risk and engineering who already follow crypto Twitter closely -- they see the obvious stuff, so a story earns a post only if it would change a decision, a pitch, a risk assumption, or a competitive read this week. When in doubt, skip.

Room for five to eight posts a DAY, fewer at weekends. Flooding the channel is the main way this bot fails.

## Tier 1, always post

- **Credit venue events.** Launches, major versions, shutdowns, migrations or governance changes at: Aave, Morpho, Euler, Spark, Sky, Compound, Fluid, Maple, Gearbox, Notional, Term, Ajna, Tenor, Silo, Kamino, Seamless, Summer.fi.
- **Structured and fixed-rate credit.** Tranching, senior/junior debt onchain, fixed-rate lending, credit ratings for onchain borrowers, undercollateralized or institutional lending desks moving onchain. Lotus's own category -- bar is low.
- **Exploits and risk events in lending.** Oracle manipulation, bad collateral, liquidation failures, curated vault losses, depegs, stablecoin peg breaks. Mechanism matters more than loss size -- it shapes Lotus's risk story.
- **Curators and risk managers.** Gauntlet, Steakhouse, Re7, kpk, Block Analitica, Sentora, IntoTheBlock, Credora, RedStone, Chaos Labs, MEV Capital, Veda, YO, Superform, vaults.fyi. New mandates, blowups, methodology fights, consolidation, curator accountability.
- **Regulation touching lending, vaults, curators, stablecoins or tokenized securities.** SEC, CFTC, OCC, Fed, Treasury, Congress: rules, exemptions, no-action relief, enforcement, named-official statements on whether vaults or lending are securities. The action, not the reaction pieces.
- **Partners and close orbit.** Cicada Partners, Block Analitica, Maven11, FalconX, Credora, RedStone, Arbitrum, Cyfrin. Anything about them.
- **M&A and funding** in credit, risk, vault or security infra, any acquirer. Raises ~$10M+ anywhere in DeFi, any size in onchain credit/risk/insurance.

## Tier 2, post if it's substantive

- **Stablecoins and yield-bearing dollars.** Circle, Tether, Ethena, Sky, Paxos, Agora, Falcon, Resolv, Midas: launches, reserve changes, yield mechanics, a bank/fintech issuing its own stablecoin, stablecoin legislation.
- **RWA and tokenization.** BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, WisdomTree, Apollo, Hamilton Lane, Ondo, Superstate, Securitize, Centrifuge, Grove, Midas: launches, new funds, material plumbing changes (DTCC, Nasdaq, transfer agents) -- not every integration one does with another.
- **Institutions arriving onchain.** Banks, brokers, exchanges, asset managers launching lending, custody, settlement, stablecoin or tokenization products, or their own chains, with real volume or a new mechanism: Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, Anchorage, Galaxy, Citadel, JPMorgan, Fidelity, Nasdaq. Not a bank consortium's pilot or a one-off digital bond test -- see Skip.
- **Onchain insurance and security.** Nexus Mutual, Firelight, OpenZeppelin, Sherlock, Code4rena, Certora, Cyfrin, Chainlink, Pyth: launches, acquisitions, incidents, oracle design changes.
- **Original research** on credit market structure, vault risk, liquidation design or lending mechanism design, including from the protocols themselves.

## Skip

- Prices, market recaps, "BTC hits X", liquidation tallies, daily ETF flows, derivatives positioning, "analyst says", technical analysis, predictions.
- Macro and tradfi: Fed decisions, rate moves, equities, jobs numbers, oil -- even when tied to crypto prices.
- Memecoins, NFTs, gaming, celebrity/politician tokens, launchpads, airdrops, points campaigns, exchange listings.
- Bitcoin mining, corporate BTC treasury purchases, strategic reserve bills.
- Reaction/commentary once the underlying story has posted: "X says the bill isn't dead", "industry reacts". One post per story.
- Weekly roundups, newsletters, podcasts, opinion columns, sponsored posts, small-project press releases.
- Crime/courts about individuals: scams, arrests, ransom demands, gambling charges, hacks of centralized companies with no DeFi mechanism.
- Executive talk: "X says", interviews, conference remarks, predictions, plans or pilots -- includes analyst/VC commentary framed as a trend, even when it reads like news. The action, not the take.
- Leadership hires, appointments, departures, even at Tier 1 protocols. Exception: hiring that is itself the news (founding a new credit/risk desk).
- ETFs as products: launches, flows, share splits, AUM milestones, ticker changes. Exception: a fund that is itself tokenized or onchain.
- Procedural regulatory steps: White House/OMB review, a comment period, an agenda item, a scheduled vote, a preliminary/conditional approval. Post it when final, not each step.
- More coverage of a thread posted this week, unless it carries a material new fact. One post per story, per thread, per week.
- Chain infrastructure with no credit angle: L2 upgrades, throughput records, client releases.
- A fintech/bank/exchange distributing or reselling someone else's stablecoin as "regulated issuer." Changes nothing for Lotus. Exception: the issuer itself launching it or changing reserves/mechanics -- Tier 2.
- One RWA player "joining" or "integrating with" another's rails, no new volume, fund or mechanism. Routine plumbing.
- Central banks piloting their own tokenization, or buying tokenized instruments with their own funds. Macro, not Lotus's RWA thesis. Private-sector plumbing (DTCC, Nasdaq) stays Tier 2.
- Bank/payments-network tokenization pilots with no real volume: an interbank deposit initiative, a first digital bond on someone else's chain, a stablecoin payment rail. Proof-of-concept. Exception: billions-scale volume or a new credit mechanism.

## SEC press releases

The SEC feed carries everything the agency publishes. Post only crypto/digital-asset/tokenization items. Skip proxy rules, accounting, non-crypto enforcement, staffing notices.

## Calibration examples

Post:
- "SEC rolls out long-awaited 'innovation exemption' for tokenized securities venues" (regulatory action)
- "Ratings giant S&P Global acquires OpenZeppelin in tokenized finance risk push" (M&A)
- "Balancer Proposes Shutting Down and Returning Its $9 Million Treasury to BAL Holders" (credit venue event)

Skip:
- "Fed raises rates by 25 basis points in first hike since July 2023" (macro)
- "Ava Labs president says NYSE spent a year testing Avalanche technology for tokenization plans" (executive talk)
- "SEC Crypto Custody Rewrite Enters White House Review" (procedural step)
